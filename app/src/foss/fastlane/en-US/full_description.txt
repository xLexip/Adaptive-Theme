<p>
    <b>Adaptive Theme</b> switches Android's system-wide light and dark mode according to the ambient brightness around you
    instead of relying on sunset or a fixed schedule. Use light mode in bright surroundings and dark mode in low light,
    with the threshold under your control.
</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Uses the device's physical ambient light sensor</li>
    <li>Custom lux threshold and presets for indoor, outdoor, and daylight conditions</li>
    <li>Wallpaper Sync for separate light and dark home and lock screen wallpapers</li>
    <li>Optional Night Lock to keep dark mode active during a chosen time period</li>
    <li>Quick Settings tile for convenient control</li>
    <li>Material You interface and more than 50 languages</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Efficient by design</b></p>
<p>
    The sensor is sampled briefly after the screen turns on rather than monitored continuously. Before changing the
    theme, Adaptive Theme checks that the sensor is not covered by a hand or pocket. Theme changes therefore happen at
    screen-on and do not interrupt you while actively using another app.
</p>
<p><b>One-time setup</b></p>
<p>
    Android does not normally allow regular apps to change system settings like the system theme. Adaptive Theme therefore needs a one-time
    grant of the <b>WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS</b> permission. This permission only allows switching system settings like the system theme and does NOT grant root access or allow the app to read any user data. 
    It is only used to switch the light/dark theme and can be revoked by uninstalling the app.
</p>
<p>The guided setup supports multiple options, choose your's:</p>
<ul>
    <li>WebADB with a simple website from a computer or secondary Android device, without installing ADB</li>
    <li>Shizuku and compatible Shizuku forks; only required once while granting the permission</li>
    <li>Root on supported devices</li>
    <li>Or a manual ADB command: adb shell pm grant dev.lexip.hecate android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS
</li>
</ul>
<p><b>More</b></p>
<p>
    Adaptive Theme requires Android 14 or newer. Tablets are currently not supported.
</p>
